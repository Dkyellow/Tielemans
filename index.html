1

Consultation

Submission

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does company registration take?

Typically, company registration in Zimbabwe takes between 3 to 7 working days, depending on the Registrar's workload.

What documents do I need to register?

You generally need copies of national IDs for all directors/shareholders and their current addresses.

Do you help with Tax Clearance?

Yes, we apply for tax clearances for both new and renewal for already registered corporates.
